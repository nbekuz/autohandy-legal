Security & Confidentiality

Maxfiylik va xavfsizlik · AutoHandy Master · Last updated: May 22, 2026

This document explains how AutoHandy Master protects your account, customer information, and business data. By using the app, you agree to handle confidential information responsibly as described below.

1. Account security

  • Sign-in uses phone verification (one-time codes). Do not share codes with anyone.
  • Access tokens are stored in secure device storage, not in plain text.
  • Sign out on shared devices. Report suspected unauthorized access immediately.

2. Confidential customer information

When you accept an order, you may see customer names, phone numbers, addresses, vehicle details, photos, and chat messages. You agree to:

  • Use this information only to perform the requested service.
  • Not copy, sell, or disclose customer data outside the AutoHandy platform.
  • Not contact customers for unrelated marketing without platform rules and consent.
  • Delete or avoid storing unnecessary screenshots of personal data on personal devices.

3. Payment & financial security

  • Bank and identity details for payouts are submitted to Stripe according to their security standards.
  • We do not ask for card numbers from customers in the Master app; card payments are handled by the platform checkout flow.
  • Never share your Stripe login, API keys, or payout credentials with third parties.

4. Communications & media

  • In-app chat and call-related features are for order support only.
  • Photos and files you upload (workshop, job completion, documents) must be relevant and lawful.
  • Do not upload malware or content that violates others’ rights.

5. Location data

Location is used for routing, arrival status, and service area features. Disable location in system settings when you are off duty if you do not want the app to access GPS.

6. Device & network safety

  • Keep your phone OS and AutoHandy Master app updated.
  • Avoid using the app on unsecured public Wi‑Fi for sensitive actions when possible.
  • Enable device lock (PIN, biometrics) to protect access if your phone is lost.

7. Incident reporting

If you notice a security issue, data leak, or misuse of customer information, contact us promptly: support@autohandy.com. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including restricting accounts when necessary.

8. Your responsibilities as a provider

  • Maintain accurate workshop and service information.
  • Complete orders honestly; fraudulent activity may lead to suspension and legal action.
  • Comply with applicable laws for automotive repair and consumer protection in your region.

9. Updates

We may revise these security and confidentiality terms. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
