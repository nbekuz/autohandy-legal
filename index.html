Privacy Policy

AutoHandy Master · Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AutoHandy Master (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application as a service technician or workshop provider connected to the AutoHandy platform.

1. Who we are

AutoHandy Master is the provider-facing application for AutoHandy. It helps you receive and manage repair orders, communicate with customers, navigate to job locations, manage your workshop profile, and receive payouts for completed work.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: phone number, name, email (if provided), profile photo, workshop address, and role (Master).
  • Verification: one-time codes sent by SMS for sign-in and account security.
  • Location: GPS coordinates when you share your position, navigate to a customer, or update your service area — only with your permission.
  • Order & work data: accepted jobs, schedules, photos you upload, chat messages, service items, reviews, and completion records.
  • Payment data: payout setup and transaction history are processed via Stripe Connect; we do not store full bank account numbers on our servers.
  • Device data: push notification tokens (Firebase), app version, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use your information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Match you with customer orders (including urgent/SOS and custom requests).
  • Enable in-app chat, maps, and order status updates.
  • Calculate performance metrics (ratings, acceptance/completion rates, earnings summaries).
  • Process payouts and show checkout/payment history.
  • Send service-related push notifications (new orders, messages, status changes).
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing with third parties

We may share data only as needed to run the service, including with:

  • Customers involved in your active orders (name, contact, job details you choose to share).
  • Stripe for Connect onboarding, balances, and payouts.
  • Google Maps / Directions for routing and map display.
  • Firebase for push messaging.
  • Cloud hosting and API providers that process data on our behalf under contractual safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Retention

We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active and as required for accounting, disputes, and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account subject to outstanding orders and payout obligations.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Update profile information in the app.
  • Control location and notification permissions in device settings.
  • Contact support to access, correct, or delete personal data where applicable by law.

7. Children

The app is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on our website. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@autohandy.com
